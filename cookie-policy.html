Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Last Updated: September 2, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how our website uses cookies and browser local storage (`localStorage`) to deliver an optimal, personalized experience.

1. Essential Functional Local Storage

We do not track you across third-party websites or sell user profiling data. Our site relies on lightweight browser storage for the following essential features:

Key Name Type Purpose & Duration
portfolio_theme localStorage Remembers your preference for Dark or Light theme mode (Persistent).
portfolio_country localStorage Stores selected country and regional currency (Persistent).
portfolio_form_rate_limit localStorage Tracks contact form submission timestamps to prevent bot abuse (5 min window).
portfolio_cookie_consent localStorage Stores your cookie consent preference choice (Persistent).

2. Managing Your Preferences

You can clear or disable browser local storage and cookies at any time in your browser settings. Essential local storage key deletion will reset visual themes to default dark mode.
