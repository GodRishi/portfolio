Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how Rishi Saha Development ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you interact with our website and lead intake systems.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide through our contact intake form, including your name, email address, project requirements, budget selection, and country/currency preference.

2. How We Use Information

  • To respond to custom website development inquiries and client quotes.
  • To estimate project timelines and regional currency rates accurately.
  • To protect our forms against automated bot submission and spam via client rate limiting and honeypot traps.

3. Data Protection & Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal details. Form submissions are routed securely via Web3Forms or FormSubmit APIs directly to our encrypted inbox.

4. Local Storage Usage

Our website utilizes local storage (`localStorage`) solely for non-tracking essential preferences: dark/light theme choice, country/currency selector, cookie consent state, and rate-limiting security timestamps.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Under GDPR and CCPA, you have the right to request deletion or modification of your personal correspondence at any time by reaching out via our contact form.
